### Functions
- Rear position lamp (R7/R148), constant intensity. The callout '4 to 17 cd on axis' implies category R1 (code R).
- Stop lamp (R7/R148), constant intensity. The callout '60 to 185 cd on axis' implies category S1 (single level), not S2 variable intensity.
- Reversing lamp (R23/R148), white. The category (AR) is implied by the function; no value is stated except visibility to 45° outboard.
- Rear retro-reflector (R3/R150). The class is not stated; Class IA or IB is implied for a vehicle rear retro-reflector, and red is implied by its rear position behind the red lens.
- Outer lens: light transmission and colour definition (red for the rear position and stop functions).

- Seal / gasket: TPE or silicone. Seals the lens to the housing against water and dust ingress.

- Housing: PP or ASA/PC. The lamp body that carries the PCB, reflector, seal and lens.
- Fasteners / clips: PA, POM or steel. Attach the lamp to the vehicle and assemble it, and keep the lamp aimed under vibration.
